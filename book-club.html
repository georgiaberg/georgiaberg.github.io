In Search of Lost Time

The required readings will be about eighty pages a week, from the first line of Swann's Way to the last of Time Regained. Each meeting falls at one of Proust's own breaks, so we always talk about a whole movement of the book. Reading begins on Sunday, January 3.

II.

A Secular Bible Study

A historical reading of the Hebrew Bible and the New Testament. Each week you watch one lecture, about fifty minutes, from Yale's free courses by Christine Hayes and Dale B. Martin, and read the passages that go with it.

Any modern translation with notes will do; the New Oxford Annotated Bible is a good one. Lectures begin the week of January 10.
